# Direct Rush

**A fast-paced **Unity** puzzle-matcher developed as a collaborative school project.**

Engage in high-speed gameplay where precision meets chaos. There is a unique **Fever System**: once activated, every match triggers powerful beams that strike and weaken the shields. Shatter the defenses to unlock new puzzle pieces and progress through the game.
